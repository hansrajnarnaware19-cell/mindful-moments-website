01☼
Grounding

Try the 5–4–3–2–1 exercise

Gently bring your attention to the present by noticing your surroundings.

  1. Name 5 things you can see.
  2. Notice 4 things you can feel.
  3. Listen for 3 things you can hear.
  4. Notice 2 things you can smell.
  5. Name 1 thing you can taste, or one thing you appreciate.

Skip any step that feels uncomfortable.

02〰
Breathing

Take a slow breathing break

Sit comfortably if you can. Breathe gently without forcing a deep breath. Let your exhale be easy and unhurried.

Try the guided activity on the dashboard for a short, timed practice. Stop if you feel dizzy or uncomfortable and return to your normal breathing.

03✎
Reflection

Write without judging

Set aside two minutes and finish one of these prompts:

  • Right now, I feel…
  • Something that has been on my mind is…
  • One small thing I need today is…

You do not have to solve everything you write down.

04☾
Daily habits

Build a softer routine

Small basics can support well-being: drink water, eat regularly when possible, get some daylight, move in a way that feels comfortable, and make time to rest.

Pick just one realistic action rather than trying to change everything at once.

When self-help isn't enough

Self-help tools are not a replacement for professional care. If your feelings are affecting daily life or feel too difficult to manage alone, reach out to a qualified mental health professional or someone you trust. In India, Tele-MANAS provides mental health support at 14416. If there is immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
